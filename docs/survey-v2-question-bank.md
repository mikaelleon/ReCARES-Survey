# Draft v2 question bank

Status: DRAFT. English only. Generated from `survey/draftInstrumentV2.ts` (instrument v2, draft). Not published; v1 stays live.

How to read this file:

- **Source** is `v1` for a question carried over unchanged from the live questionnaire and `new` for a question added in v2.
- **Type** is the question type the survey supports. Likert questions use one of two 5-point scales: agreement (Strongly disagree to Strongly agree) or likelihood (Very unlikely to Very likely).
- **Shown to** lists the resident types that can see the question. The six types are the answers to A1: 1 owner living in the unit, 2 OFW owner, 3 absentee owner, 4 household member of a homeowner, 5 tenant or lessee, 6 household member of a tenant. The text after the types is the extra condition.
- **What it shows in the synthesis** is the finding the answers support in the paper.
- Items marked **(reverse)** are negatively worded; flip them before combining with positively worded items.
- **Level**: every rated category shows 5 Likert questions by default (6 in F, because F7 is a core code). The other Likert questions are Optional: they appear only after an earlier answer signals a reason to ask. Non-Likert questions are Default, or Conditional when a gate applies.
- A signal is an answer that is not clearly favorable: Neutral, Disagree or Strongly disagree on a favorably worded statement, Neutral, Agree or Strongly agree on a negatively worded one. F items signal on interest (Likely or Very likely). Not applicable never signals.
- Some optional items sit on mutually exclusive branches (for example R8 and R9), so no respondent sees all ten Likert items in a category.
- Hidden questions are stored as `not_shown`.
