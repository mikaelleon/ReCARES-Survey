# For residents

This page is a walkthrough of the **public** ReCARES website. You do not need an account. If a word is unclear, see the [glossary](glossary.md).

The address of the live site is the hosting URL your team published (for example a Firebase Hosting domain). On a developer’s computer it is usually `http://localhost:3000`.

---

## Homepage

The homepage has five jobs, in order:

1. **Welcome (hero)** — photo of the community, the ReCARES name, and two actions: **Start the survey** and **Learn more**.
2. **What is this survey for?** — research context with the Camella Homes mark.
3. **About us** — who the student team is, and that collaboration with the HOA is still exploratory.
4. **Goals of this study** — safer private reporting, accessible services, faster security response.
5. **FAQ** — common questions, plus a side card with time estimate, voluntary note, and confidentiality.
6. **Contact** — inquiry form for the team, or **Chat with RAGbot**.

The **sticky bar** at the top stays on screen: Home, About, FAQ, Start the survey, and a moon button for dark mode. On a phone, those links sit in a menu button.

**Proponent access** is only in the footer, on purpose, so residents are not invited into the admin login by accident.

## Taking the survey

1. Choose **Start the survey**.
2. Read the welcome screen. You can continue, or resume a draft if this device already started one.
3. Complete **consent and eligibility** (you must agree before numbered questions appear).
4. Answer the household and service questions. A red asterisk means the question is required to go forward. Personal items often include **Prefer not to say**.
5. Some screens appear only if an earlier answer said they apply (for example accessibility details after you say someone in the household has a disability or mobility limit).
6. Review, then submit. You will see a thank-you page.

You can leave and come back **on the same phone or computer**. The draft is stored only on that device until you submit. Clearing the browser’s site data deletes the draft.

If this device already submitted, the welcome screen warns you. Continue only if a different person is answering.

A fuller map of screens: [The survey](survey.md).

---

## Interview invitation

Near the end, you may be asked if the team may contact you for a follow-up interview. That is optional.

- If you say **yes**, you can leave a way to reach you (for example email or phone) and preferred days or times.
- If you say **no**, you still complete the survey as usual.
- There is also a standalone interview page at `/survey/interview/` if someone needs that form by itself.

Your interview details are for the research team, not a public listing.

---

## RAGbot

RAGbot is a chat helper on the public site. Use it for questions about the survey, screening, and FAQ topics. It does **not** file an HOA complaint or talk to security.

If the chat widget does not appear, an ad blocker or browser shield may be hiding it. You can still use the inquiry form.

---

## Inquiry form

At the bottom of the homepage you can send a message to the student team. Give an email so they can reply.

Until the team finishes wiring the inquiries collection, treat this as “best effort”: if a message does not go through, use the contact path your adviser published for the live study.

---

## Language and theme

- **Theme:** the moon button switches light and dark. The choice is remembered on that browser.
- **Language:** English is the resident UI. Filipino survey strings exist in the product for later; language selection is intended at the survey consent gate, not in the site footer.

## If something feels wrong

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You are not a Camella Homes Tibig resident | You may stop. The study is written for that community. |
| You do not want to continue | Leave before Submit. Nothing is stored as a finished response until you submit. |
| You worry the HOA will see *your* answers | Individual responses are not given to the HOA with a household attached. See [Data and privacy](data-and-privacy.md). |
| You need help using the form | A trusted person may read the questions and enter the answers you choose. The survey says this on the household screens. |
